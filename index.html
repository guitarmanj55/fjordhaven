Fjordhaven

Settle a Nordic fjord. Let the village grow its own paths.

How to play
  • Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, twist with two fingers to turn.
  • Build from the bar: tap a building, tap the ground to place it, ⟳ to turn it, ✓ to build. Its door must face open ground.
  • Villagers pick their own jobs, build, fetch and eat. Tap a villager or building to see what they are up to.
  • Where villagers walk the grass wears into trails, then roads. Put shops where people already walk.
  • Homes burn a log a day in winter. Stock food and firewood in summer and autumn.
  • Content villagers with free beds send word, and a longship brings more settlers.
